## What this page must answer

- How the system exchanges dataset descriptions and access applications with the HealthData@EU central platform (for example through the national eDelivery dispatcher), and from which part of the system.
- Whether a country's 1+MG NCP node and its EHDS national contact point are the same node, separate nodes, or a national choice.
- What the system supports now, and what is target state until the HealthData@EU implementing act is adopted.
- The options considered, and what each one means for the building blocks of chapter 5.
