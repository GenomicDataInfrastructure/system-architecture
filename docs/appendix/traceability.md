# Governance traceability

This table lists every numbered section of the **1+MG Data Governance master document (version 2025-12)** and the architecture pages that implement it. The governance is published as the annex of GDI D2.4 <Cite id="dg" />, on [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/19096953). The table is generated from the `governance_refs` field of each page, so it is always up to date.

- **Bold rows** are chapters and steps of the governance.
- **Applies to** shows the notes in square brackets in the governance titles: the types of use or the types of dataset a section is limited to. Types of dataset (1+MG compliant, 1+MG cohort) are highlighted.
- The detailed responsibility sections (chapters V to VIII) are the requirements the architecture must cover. When no page references one, it is marked as a **gap**.
- To cite a section in a page, write `<GovRef id="VII.2.3" />`. The link opens the governance document at the section heading (or at its page, in PDF viewers that can't jump to a heading); so does the section number in the first column below. It opens this site's unchanged copy of the PDF published on [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/19096953) (CC BY 4.0), because Zenodo sends the file as a download and the browser then can't jump to the page.
- Sections are identified by their number, never by a page number. The published document numbers the overview section IV and its subsections as I. and I.1 to I.4; the table shows this next to the title.
